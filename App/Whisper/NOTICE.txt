WhisperLogMel.swift is a Swift/Accelerate adaptation of that repository's
apps/VoxScribe/Flutter/lib/services/log_mel.dart. The adaptation replaces
Dart arithmetic loops with Accelerate dot products and pads recordings to
the deployed model's fixed 30-second input shape.
